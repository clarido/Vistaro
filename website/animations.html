Every animation, played live

A Vistaro timeline is a list of steps, and each step has an action. This page plays every one of them on the same mockup, and you can pause, step through or replay any clip. The whole page is one screen (showcase/index.html) and twelve small timelines in this repo.

anim.config.json · 9 steps · 20 s

Everything at once

The 20-second overview: a highlight, a real click, character-by-character typing, a camera push, a staggered chart build and a closing fade. One timeline, in the order you'd tell the story.

Interactions
"action": "click"

Click

The cursor travels with a human drift, a spotlight frames the target, a ripple marks the press. Then a real el.click() fires, so the page's own handlers react.

"action": "type"

Type

Text lands character by character at a human pace, through real key events. React-style controlled inputs see genuine keystrokes, not a pasted value.

"action": "focus"

Focus

The element takes the page's real focus, so its own focus ring lights up. Works on anything focusable: inputs, buttons, links.

"action": "hover"

Hover

The cursor glides onto an element and its :hover styles respond. The lift, the shadow and the color change all come from the mockup's own CSS.

"action": "press"

Press

No cursor and no target, just the keyboard: Enter, Tab, or a chord like Control+K. Where type fills the field, press submits it.

"action": "highlight"

Highlight

A spotlight pulse that says notice this. Nothing gets clicked and nothing changes. The ring sizes itself to whatever you point at.

Camera & movement
"action": "camera"

Camera

Push in on a detail, glide to the next one without a cut, then pull back to the full frame. The cursor and spotlight ride along.

"action": "scroll"

Scroll

Smooth-scroll a buried element into view, inside any scrollable container or the page itself.

Reveals & motion
"action": "fadeIn"

Fade in

Reveal anything hidden in CSS on cue: a result card, a toast, or the whole page as an opening beat. display and opacity are handled together.

"action": "transitionScreen"

Transition screen

Cross-fade between two screens authored in one file. You get a multi-screen story with no real page load, and every other action keeps working on the new screen.

"action": "animate"

Animate

The free-form one. It animates from a from state to a to state with Web Animations: transforms that compose, any CSS property, every match of a selector with a stagger, and seven named easings from linear to spring.
